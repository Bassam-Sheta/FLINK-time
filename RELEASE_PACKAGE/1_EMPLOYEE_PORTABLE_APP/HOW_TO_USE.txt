========================================================== 
ULTRA-ACCOUNT EMPLOYEE PORTABLE APP 
========================================================== 
1. Keep UltraAccount.exe and workspace.json in the same folder. 
2. Double-click UltraAccount.exe (or Launch_Portable_Tracker.bat). 
3. The Clockify-style portable client will launch immediately. 
4. Click 'PROFILE & SETTINGS' or the top profile badge to set your email.
